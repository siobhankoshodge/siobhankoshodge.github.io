Start small · AI Workflow Review

One task.
A practical way forward.

A focused review for a small-business owner who wants help applying AI to a real task. We begin with what you do now, the tools you can access and the result you need.

What you take away

  • A simple process tailored to one agreed task.
  • A reusable prompt or template to get you started.
  • A short screen-recorded walkthrough you can revisit.
  • A plain-English guide to checking the result.
  • One round of follow-up questions.

We can work mainly through written messages and a recorded walkthrough. We’ll agree the scope, price and delivery date before any paid work begins.

Ask about a Workflow Review

  1. Tell me what’s getting in the way

    Describe one task and the tools you use. A general example is enough to start.

  2. Check what will actually work

    We’ll check your AI access, what information you can use and whether a small improvement is realistic. If it needs a different tool or a bigger project, I’ll explain that first.

  3. Try it in your own account

    You receive a walkthrough and a reusable starting point. Try the process with your own tools and bring back your questions.

  4. Keep what helps

    Check the output and compare the effort with your usual approach. The aim is a useful habit you can keep using independently.

A few questions you might have

Before you get started.

Do I need to know anything about AI?

You can start as a complete beginner. Tell me what you want help with in your own words, and we’ll work from there.

What if I only have a free AI account?

That’s useful to know at the start. We’ll check what your particular account supports and what your business permits. We’ll discuss any additional access or costs before agreeing the work.

Will the task run automatically afterwards?

The Workflow Review focuses on a process you can use yourself. You may still need to provide information, run a prompt and check the result. Connecting systems or running tasks automatically would need a separate scope.

Do we need to arrange a daytime call?

Most of the review can happen through written messages and a short recorded walkthrough. We’ll agree how to work together and the delivery date before starting.

Should I send you business documents straight away?

Start with a general description. Please don’t send passwords, customer records or confidential documents in an initial enquiry. If an example is needed, we’ll agree what is appropriate to share.
